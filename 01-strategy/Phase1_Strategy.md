## 3. Competitor findings

**CGI (Winnipeg expansion page, 3 Dec 2025).** CGI announced acquiring Online Business Systems, a Winnipeg IT consultancy founded in 1986 with 350+ staff, and frames the pitch as "local relationships with global scale". Headline specialties: digital transformation, cybersecurity, AI. The page is a corporate press release: no booking path, no interactivity, heavy legal text.
- Lesson: lead with named specialties and a local-plus-reach story.
- Opening for Ideovate: a boutique can be faster, more personal and more specialised. CGI cannot credibly claim "AI plus ISO quality system certification support" in one small, senior team.
- Risk: consolidation means larger players are entering the Winnipeg market. Differentiate on specialisation, not size.

**IntegrationWorx.** The site blocks automated reading, so I could not analyse it and did not work around that. Please open it yourself and tell me 3 things: the hero headline, what the main button says, and what you admire most. I will add it to this document.

**National ISO 42001 landscape.** Search results show large firms (for example PwC Canada, with a launch announcement for AI governance certification, and SGS and CertPro in an AI governance directory) already compete on ISO 42001. I have only seen titles and links, not the pages, so treat this as a signal to verify.
- Opening: those firms target large enterprises. SMEs and mid-market organisations in Canada and the US look underserved. Position Ideovate as the practical, affordable route.

**Local AI search.** Results for "AI consulting Winnipeg" were dominated by one national content-heavy site (chatgpt.ca) using location pages. Lesson: local landing pages work. Plan Winnipeg and Manitoba pages.
