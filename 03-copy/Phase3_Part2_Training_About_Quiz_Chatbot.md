## K. ISO READINESS QUIZ

- H1: **How ready are you for ISO?** Ten questions. Two minutes. A clear picture at the end.
- Start choice: Which standard? (9001, 14001, 45001, 27001, 42001, Not sure.) Same 10 questions for all, with the wording adapted by the standard.
- Scoring per question: **Yes, fully = 3 · Partly = 2 · Planned = 1 · No / Not sure = 0.** Total out of 30.

| # | Question (adapt "management system" to the chosen standard) |
|---|---|
| 1 | Is there a named leader responsible for this management system? |
| 2 | Have you defined the scope and the processes it covers? |
| 3 | Do you have written policies and objectives that staff can find? |
| 4 | Do you identify and record risks and opportunities? |
| 5 | Are key procedures documented and followed? |
| 6 | Do you train staff on their responsibilities? |
| 7 | Do you monitor performance with measures you review? |
| 8 | Have you run an internal audit in the last 12 months? |
| 9 | Does management review results and decide on improvements? |
| 10 | Do you have a way to record and fix problems (corrective actions)? |

**Result bands (shown on screen and emailed):**
- **0 to 10: Early stage.** "You have room to shape this properly from the start. A gap assessment would give you a clear first plan."
- **11 to 20: Building momentum.** "You have foundations in place. A focused gap assessment can show what remains and in what order."
- **21 to 30: Nearly there.** "You're well prepared. A readiness review can confirm the last gaps before an audit."
- All bands: **Book a free consultation to talk through your result.** Show the three questions they scored lowest as "Where to focus first".
- Disclaimer: "This quiz gives a general readiness indication. It is not an audit and does not predict the outcome of a registrar assessment."
- Capture: name, email, company, consent to be contacted. Send to CRM with the standard, score and answers.

## L. CHATBOT CONCIERGE (script)

**Name:** Ideovate Assistant. **Greeting:** "Hi, I'm the Ideovate Assistant. I can answer questions about our AI, ISO and training services, or help you book a free consultation. What brings you here?"
**Quick replies:** Build AI · ISO readiness · Training · Book a consultation.

**Can do:** explain services in plain language, point to the right page, start the quiz, recommend a course category, collect name, email and need, and book a consultation.
**Must not:** quote prices or timelines as promises, guarantee any audit or registration outcome, give legal or compliance advice, make claims about clients or results not on the site, or share private data.
**Standard replies**
- Pricing: "Every project is different, so we scope it first. A free consultation is the quickest way to get a clear answer. Shall I help you book one?"
- Guarantees: "We prepare and support you thoroughly, but registrars make the final decision. I can't promise an outcome."
- Out of scope: "I'm not the right source for that. I can pass your question to the team. May I take your email?"
- Handoff: "I'll pass this to Vivek or Mamta and they'll reply within one business day." Capture name, email, question; send to CRM.
**Privacy line (first message):** "Please don't share sensitive information in this chat."
