# Ideovate design system (recorded from the built homepage)

**World:** AI engineering held inside governance. Dark ink night (hero, story, process, close) turning to warm paper (paths, services, industries). One idea per band; ruled rows instead of cards.

**Colour:** Ink #0A0B14 / #12142A; Paper #F7F5F0; Spark cyan #22D3EE (actions, emphasis word); Pulse violet #7C5CFF (orbit, process line); Trust gold #C8A24A (hairline above the registrar note, orbit dot); Spark-deep #07687C for links and icons on paper. Contrast checked at AA.
**Type:** Sora (display, 600 to 700, tracking -0.02em, hero max 5.5rem) and Inter (text, 17px, measure 52 to 60ch). Loaded from Google Fonts at runtime.
**Surfaces:** hairline rules (1px) not boxes; no cards; buttons are pills with an offset shadow; no gradient text; no halos.
**Motion:** one easing, expo-out (0.22, 1, 0.36, 1); 150/300/700 ms. Authored moments: the mark forming, the cursor-reactive node field, the scroll-scrubbed process line, service hairlines and icons drawing on entry. Rows on the paths band wipe to ink on hover. All motion off under prefers-reduced-motion.
**Components:** header with scrolled state and mobile drawer; hero with node field and legend; path rows; ruled service lists (grouped); timeline; chips; close band; footer.
**Logo:** Spark Orbit (`02-brand/logos`). Simplified mark under 40 px.
**Placeholders to replace:** hours, consultation length, booking link (`/book/` is a holding page), proof figures, founder photos, social links.
